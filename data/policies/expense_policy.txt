ACME TECH PVT LTD - TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

1. Submitting claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, through the finance portal, with original bills or receipts attached. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected.

2. Meals during business travel
Meals are reimbursed up to Rs 600 per day during approved business travel. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

3. Local travel
Cab and auto fares for client visits are reimbursed with a receipt or app invoice. Employees should use the cheapest reasonable option.

4. Flights and hotels
Flights must be booked in economy class. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to Rs 5,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 3,500 per night in other cities.

5. Approvals
Claims up to Rs 10,000 are approved by the reporting manager. Claims above Rs 10,000 also need approval from the department director.

6. Payment
Approved claims are paid within 7 working days of approval, into the salary bank account.
