ACME TECH PVT LTD - IT AND INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

1. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must be changed every 90 days. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff.

2. Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all company applications.

3. Lost or stolen devices
If a laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the security team at security@acmetech.example within 2 hours. The device will be locked remotely.

4. Data handling
Company data must not be stored on personal drives, personal email or personal cloud accounts. USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.

5. Software
Only software approved by IT may be installed. Requests for new software go through the IT service desk.

6. Phishing
Report suspicious emails to security@acmetech.example. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders.
