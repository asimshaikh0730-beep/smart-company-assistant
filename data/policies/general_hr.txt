ACME TECH PVT LTD - HOLIDAYS, WORKING HOURS AND GENERAL HR

1. Working hours
Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.

2. Public holidays
Acme Tech gives 12 paid public holidays every year. These include Republic Day (26 January), Holi, Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October), Diwali (2 days) and Christmas (25 December). The full list is published by HR every January.

3. Probation
New employees are on probation for 3 months. Confirmation is based on the manager's review at the end of probation.

4. Notice period
The notice period for confirmed employees is 60 days. During probation the notice period is 15 days.

5. Salary date
Salary is credited on the last working day of every month.

6. HR contact
For any HR question, write to hr@acmetech.example.
