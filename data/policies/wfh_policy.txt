ACME TECH PVT LTD - WORK FROM HOME (HYBRID) POLICY

1. Hybrid schedule
Acme Tech follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office 3 days a week, on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are work-from-home days.

2. Core hours
Whether in office or at home, employees must be available online during core hours, 11:00 to 16:00 IST.

3. Home internet allowance
Employees receive a work-from-home allowance of Rs 1,000 per month, paid with the salary, to cover internet costs. No bills are needed.

4. Extended remote work
Working fully remotely for more than 5 consecutive working days (for example, from a hometown) needs written approval from the manager and HR at least 7 days in advance.

5. Equipment
Laptops and monitors issued by the company must be used for all work. Employees are responsible for the safe keeping of company equipment at home.
