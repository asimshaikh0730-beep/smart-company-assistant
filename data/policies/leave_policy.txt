ACME TECH PVT LTD - LEAVE POLICY

1. Leave entitlement
Every full-time employee gets 18 days of annual leave, 8 days of sick leave and 6 days of casual leave in each calendar year. Leave is credited on 1 January.

2. Applying for leave
Annual leave of more than 2 days must be applied at least 3 working days in advance. Casual leave can be applied one day before. Sick leave can be applied on the same day or after returning. Your manager approves or rejects a request within 2 working days.

3. Medical certificate
If sick leave is taken for more than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered doctor must be submitted to HR within 3 days of returning to work.

4. Carry forward and encashment
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Unused annual leave beyond 5 days is encashed in the January salary. Sick leave and casual leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

5. Maternity and paternity leave
Female employees get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees get 10 days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 3 months of the birth of the child.

6. Unpaid leave
Leave without pay needs written approval from the manager and the HR head. It cannot exceed 30 days in a year.
